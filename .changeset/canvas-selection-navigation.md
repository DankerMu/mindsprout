Select canvas nodes with click, Shift+click, or an enclosing drag rectangle. Use Ctrl/Cmd+A to select visible nodes, Escape to clear selection, and arrow keys to navigate from the last-clicked primary node. Text focus and composition keep their native shortcuts; panning preserves selection. Selection is session-only and does not save or alter the map.

Space panning preserves selection even when focus is outside the canvas and resets on window blur. Drag rectangles accept only the primary left mouse pointer; touch gestures remain available for panning.
