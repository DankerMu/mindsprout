Rename maps from the library with an explicit title form, or permanently delete them after confirmation. Rename updates the title and last-updated time without changing the tree or creating an undo step. Successful renames move into the library's newest-first order; failed mutations retain the saved row and allow an explicit retry.

Newer-version maps refuse rename with “此导图由更新版本创建”. Every listed map can be explicitly deleted, including newer or odd-version records, without reading or migrating its contents. Records without readable summary metadata remain hidden and cannot be deleted through the library.
